# Governance Participation Guide

How XDC community members can follow and take part in network governance. For background on the DAO Treasury and its role in the DAOFIN ecosystem, start with the [Governance Overview](/docs/xdc-chain/governance/overview).

---

## Background: The DAO Treasury

XDC Network governance operates through the [XDCDAO](https://www.xdcdao.org/) framework. The DAO Treasury manages the community's collective resources and funds:

- Protocol enhancements
- Community-driven projects
- Network security initiatives
- Ecosystem expansion

Treasury spending is governed by the community rather than a single entity, which makes transparent tracking and broad participation essential.

---

## Tracking Proposals

Proposals and votes for the XDCDAO are hosted on the official DAO platform:

- **[xdcdao.org](https://www.xdcdao.org/)** — the primary portal for active proposals, voting status, and treasury information.

Check the portal regularly to stay current on what's being decided and when voting windows open and close.

---

## Voting Process

Voting takes place on the XDCDAO platform. At a high level:

1. **Follow the discussion** — Proposals are discussed in community channels before and during voting (see below).
2. **Review the proposal** — Read the full proposal text, requested funding (if any), and expected impact.
3. **Cast your vote** — Connect your wallet on [xdcdao.org](https://www.xdcdao.org/) and vote on active proposals while the voting window is open.

:::note
These docs currently cover the DAO Treasury at a high level only. Detailed proposal mechanics (quorum, thresholds, proposal submission steps) are defined by the DAOFIN framework and the XDCDAO platform itself — consult [xdcdao.org](https://www.xdcdao.org/) for the authoritative process. This page will be expanded as more governance detail is documented.
:::

## Discussion Channels

Governance proposals are debated in the same channels as the wider XDC community:

| Channel | Link | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Developer Forum | [www.xdc.dev](https://www.xdc.dev) | Detailed, long-form proposal discussion |
| Discord | [discord.gg/xdc](https://discord.gg/xdc) | Real-time discussion and questions |
| Telegram | [t.me/xinfintalk](https://t.me/xinfintalk) | General community chat |
| GitHub | [github.com/XinFinOrg](https://github.com/XinFinOrg) | Technical proposals and code-level discussion |

Before voting, read the forum thread for the proposal — it's where trade-offs and community feedback are documented in the most depth.

---

## Treasury Transparency

The DAO Treasury exists to fund work that aligns with the community's goals. To follow how funds are allocated:

- Review funding proposals and their outcomes on [xdcdao.org](https://www.xdcdao.org/)
- Track approved spending and funded initiatives through announcements in the community channels above
- Ask questions about any proposal in the [Developer Forum](https://www.xdc.dev) — treasury decisions are community business
